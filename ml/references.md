
# CosmoCare AI – ML References

## 1. NASA Human Research Program

NASA identifies five major hazards of human spaceflight, including isolation and confinement. These hazards can affect astronaut health, behaviour, sleep, and performance.

Source:
https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/

## 2. NASA – Isolation and Confinement

NASA discusses how isolation and confinement can contribute to sleep loss, circadian disruption, and performance difficulties.

Source:
https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-isolation-and-confinement/

## 3. MedlinePlus – Vital Signs

Used as a general reference for adult resting vital signs, including heart rate, blood pressure, and body temperature.

Source:
https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002341.htm

## 4. CDC – Diabetes Testing

Provides reference information for fasting blood glucose levels. Normal fasting blood glucose is 99 mg/dL or below.

Source:
https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/diabetes-testing/index.html

## 5. Dataset and Simulation Disclaimer

The dataset used in this project is synthetically generated for demonstration purposes.

It contains simulated measurements for four astronauts over fourteen days. The gradual sleep and mood changes are artificially introduced to demonstrate personal-baseline monitoring and trend detection.

These records are NOT actual astronaut health data.

The simulated values and alert thresholds are intended for software demonstration, not medical diagnosis or clinical decision-making.

Sleep and mood values are illustrative simulation assumptions, not universal clinical reference ranges.
